# Sandustry — Features

Sandustry factory tools for PC with resource controls, production settings, mining assistance and world profiles. Build a test factory, compare layouts and keep your main world separate from construction experiments.

## Resource quantities

Adjust material quantities for construction and production.

## Production rates

Tune production pacing to match your factory setup.

## Mining assistance

Adjust excavation settings for mining and terrain experiments.

## Water and layout tools

Organize water-related factory setups and keep annotated blueprint layouts.

## Sandbox profiles

Save separate configurations for construction tests and ordinary progression.

## World restoration

Return to a saved world state after testing a new production line.

## Profiles

Save your world, prepare a material budget and try a different factory layout. Compare the production setup before returning to your main configuration.
